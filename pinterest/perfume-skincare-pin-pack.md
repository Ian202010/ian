## Step 5: Images, legally and cheaply

- **Best:** your own photos of products you own. These perform best and show you've used them.
- **Good:** free Canva templates in 1000×1500 with a soft background, your text overlay and your
  own product photo.
- **Don't** download Amazon's product photos. That breaks the Associates agreement.
- **Don't** use AI to generate fake photos of real branded products. It's misleading to buyers. AI
  backgrounds are fine; add "AI-assisted image" to the description if you use them.

---

## Beauty-specific rules

- **No medical claims.** Say "helps hydrate" or "for dry skin", never "cures acne",
  "removes wrinkles" or "heals eczema".
- **Only say "I love this" or "my favorite" about products you've actually used.** Fake
  personal reviews break the FTC's endorsement rules.
- **Be careful with "dupe" pins.** "Perfume dupes" is a popular search, but naming a luxury
  brand ("smells like [Brand]") can get you into trademark trouble. If you do it, only say it
  for products whose own Amazon listing describes them as "inspired by", and never imply they're
  the real brand.
